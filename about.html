Our Story

From a single desk in Birmingham to two offices

Brady Legal began the way most honest firms do: with one solicitor, one desk and a refusal to grow faster than the quality of the work could justify.

Jonathan Arron Little qualified in London and spent his early years in commercial litigation at a large City practice. The experience was formative in one respect above all others — it showed him how easily a client can become a file number. When he moved to Birmingham and set up on his own, he carried a simple ambition: a firm where the person who answers the phone is the person who knows your case.

The firm grew slowly and deliberately. The second office opened in the City in 2019, serving clients who appreciated the same approach at closer quarters. Today Brady Legal advises on matters ranging from a first flat in Solihull to a multi-million-pound acquisition in the City — and the way we treat clients is identical in both.

What We Stand For

Four values, applied daily

These are not framed posters in the waiting room. They are the standard every instruction is measured against.

I.Directness

You deal with the solicitor who runs your matter, from the first meeting to the final letter. No hand-offs, no call centres.

II.Candour

We tell you what we think, not what you might want to hear. If a claim is weak, we say so before you spend a penny.

III.Proportion

The fee should reflect the matter, never the other way round. Fixed fees are our default wherever the work allows.

IV.Discretion

Legal problems are private problems. We protect your confidence as though it were our own, because it is our duty to do so.
