01 · Commercial & Corporate

Legal advice that understands business

Contracts are written to be performed, not tested in court. We draft them that way — tightly enough to protect you, flexibly enough to be practical.

From incorporation and shareholders' agreements to share and asset purchases, we advise owners and directors on the decisions that shape their businesses. Where a relationship breaks down, we act first to resolve it commercially and resort to litigation only when it is the better financial choice.

What we handle

  • — Business sales, purchases and management buyouts
  • — Shareholders' and partnership agreements
  • — Commercial contracts, terms and conditions
  • — Franchising, agency and distribution arrangements
  • — Intellectual property and data protection
Commercial matters, London office

02 · Property & Conveyancing

Home is the biggest purchase most of us ever make

Conveyancing looks straightforward until a chain stalls, a search comes back dirty or the seller's lease turns out to contain a surprise. Our job is to find the surprises before you are committed to them.

Every residential matter runs on a fixed fee quoted before we begin, covering the work we expect and identified in writing if the matter becomes genuinely complex. Commercial property work is quoted case by case.

What we handle

  • — Purchases and sales, freehold and leasehold
  • — Remortgages and transfer of equity
  • — Lease extensions and enfranchisement
  • — New build and Help to Buy completions
  • — Commercial leases, landlord and tenant work
Fixed fees, agreed in writing

03 · Employment Law

For the employee and the employer, fairly

Employment disputes are rarely about the letter of the law alone — they are about notice periods, reputations and next jobs. We aim to resolve them before they reach a tribunal, and to prepare for the tribunal properly when they do.

We act for individuals who have been dismissed or discriminated against, and for employers who need their processes to withstand scrutiny. Settlement agreements are reviewed within forty-eight hours for a fixed fee.

What we handle

  • — Unfair and wrongful dismissal claims
  • — Discrimination and whistleblowing
  • — Settlement agreements and exit negotiations
  • — Tribunal claims and appeals, all stages
  • — Employer contracts, handbooks and restructuring
Most matters settle before the tribunal door

04 · Family Law

Handled with the care the situation demands

Family breakdown is rarely about winning. It is about dividing a shared life fairly, protecting children and reaching a position from which everyone can move forward.

We are members of Resolution, which commits us to dealing with family matters constructively and without escalating conflict. Where agreement is not possible, we prepare the case properly for court — but we never lose sight of the cost of that road, emotionally or financially.

What we handle

  • — Divorce and dissolution of civil partnerships
  • — Financial settlements and pension sharing
  • — Arrangements for children and parenting plans
  • — Cohabitation and pre-nuptial agreements
  • — Mediation, collaborative law and arbitration
Resolution-accredited practice

05 · Dispute Resolution

Litigation is a means, not an end

Anyone can issue a claim. The skill is in knowing when to, when not to, and how to hold your nerve when the other side blinks first.

We handle commercial and civil disputes with a bias towards resolution: a well-argued letter costs a fraction of a hearing, and most disputes are won or lost before proceedings ever begin. When court is unavoidable, we are steady there too.

What we handle

  • — Contract and debt disputes, all values
  • — Shareholder and partnership disputes
  • — Property and boundary disputes
  • — Insolvency and directors' duties
  • — Mediation, arbitration and court proceedings
Settle what should settle

06 · Wills, Probate & Estates

The quiet work, done properly

No one calls a solicitor about a will when life is simple. They call after a diagnosis, a birth or a death — and they deserve a firm that treats those moments with patience.

We draft wills and lasting powers of attorney, administer estates after a death, and resolve the disputes that occasionally arise between those left behind. Fees are agreed in advance and, for estate administration, a detailed estimate is provided before we begin.

What we handle

  • — Wills, codicils and trusts for inheritance tax planning
  • — Lasting powers of attorney for property and health
  • — Estate administration and grants of probate
  • — Contentious probate and inheritance claims
  • — Estate planning for expatriates and international families
Plans that protect the people you leave behind
