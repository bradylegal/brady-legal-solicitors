The Firm

Advise as you would wish to be advised.

Birmingham office · Colmore Row

Every firm promises to put its clients first. We built ours around the one thing that consistently gets lost in the transaction: being told the truth in plain English, at every stage of the matter.

Brady Legal Solicitors was established on the principle that good advice should be proportionate, honest and priced fairly. We are not a firm of three hundred lawyers where you meet a different person each time you call. You deal with the solicitor who runs your matter, from the first meeting to the final letter.

Our work spans the everyday and the exceptional — a first flat, a family settlement, a shareholder dispute, an estate after bereavement. The care is the same regardless of the size of the matter, because the stakes are always high for the person on the other end of the phone.

How We Work

Four steps, no surprises

Legal matters are stressful enough without opaque processes and invoices that arrive unexplained. This is how a matter runs with us.

01

First meeting

We listen properly, assess your position honestly, and set out the options with their likely costs. If you do not need a lawyer, we will tell you.

02

Planning

A clear fee agreement, a written strategy and a named solicitor who will stay with the matter to the end.

03

Action

Regular updates at agreed intervals. If anything changes, you hear it from us first — not from the other side.

04

Conclusion

A written report on the outcome, a final invoice that matches the estimate, and honest advice on what comes next.

Concluding matters the right way

1.Direct access

The solicitor you meet is the one who acts for you.

2.Fixed fees

Agreed in writing before any work begins.

3.Straight talk

Plain English, including when the news is unwelcome.
